WHO WE ARE

One small team, five services, for the traveler who has done loud holidays and wants the opposite.

The Quiet Pilgrim is five small services, run by one team, working quietly across Bali since 2023. One promise holds them together: nothing about your time here is generic, cold, or rushed.

THE PERSON BEHIND IT

A table that feels like home.

I cook from the heart, food that is simple, healthy, and made the way it is at home. The smell of herbs and onions in a warm kitchen is, to me, the smell of home, and that is the feeling I want to give you at your table.

HOW WE WORK

A simple handshake, then we take care of it.

01 A NOTE

You write to us

A few lines is enough. Our operations team reads email most mornings, before the day begins.

02 A SHAPE

We draft the week, gently

Nothing fixed. A rhythm, a few invitations. You move it around.

03 THE WEEK

We are there, in person

Our chefs in the kitchen. Our drivers on the road. Our practitioners at the bale. Whichever service you booked, a real pair of hands.

04 ONE NOTE BACK

A single, transparent invoice

Sent the day after you leave. Every line listed, Bali hospitality tax (PB1) included, in rupiah or dollars, whichever suits.
