A QUIET PILGRIM SERVICE

Pilgrim Adventures.

The lively days, held to the same quiet hand as the rest of the week.

WHAT IT IS

The bigger days, arranged the quiet way.

A river in the morning, a volcano before dawn, the reef water off Penida, a back lane on an old Vespa. The active days most of Bali sells as a crowd, arranged instead as a private day of your own, with your driver, a guide who knows the ground, and everything taken care of before you set off. Each one is shaped around your group and quoted quietly, the same way we plan a journey. Nothing here is a fixed package on a shelf.

ON THE WATER

A river, and the open sea.

A HALF DAY, MORNING

White water on the Ayung

A morning on the Ayung river, through the green gorge and past the carved stone walls, with a guide who knows every bend. Lively where it should be, calm where it counts.

A FULL DAY

Snorkelling with the manta rays

A boat out to the manta points, into clear water with the big, gentle rays gliding below. For confident swimmers and quiet watchers alike.

MOUNT BATUR

The volcano, at first and last light.

Two ways up, and two hours of the day. On foot for the climb, or by private jeep for the ease of it, at sunrise or at sunset.

A NIGHT START, FOR THE DAWN

Mount Batur sunrise on foot

A guided climb through the dark to reach the summit before first light, breakfast and a warm drink at the top as the sun comes up over the caldera, and a coffee garden on the way home.

AN AFTERNOON CLIMB, FOR THE SUNSET

Mount Batur sunset on foot

The same guided climb, timed for the evening. Up through the afternoon to the summit, a meal and a warm drink at the top, and the sun going down over the old black lava below.

AN EARLY START, FOR THE SUNRISE

Mount Batur sunrise by jeep

A four in the morning start up the volcano by private jeep, breakfast and a hot drink at the top as the sky lightens, the old black lava fields below, and a photographer along to catch it.

LATE AFTERNOON, FOR THE SUNSET

Mount Batur sunset by jeep

The same private jeep up the volcano, timed for the evening. Across the old black lava fields, a meal and a hot drink at the top, and the sun going down over the caldera.

ON TWO WHEELS, AND THROUGH THE MUD

The back lanes, at your own pace.

A HALF DAY, MORNING

An ATV through the mud and jungle

A quad through the Ubud jungle on an easy semi-automatic bike, along muddy back trails, a river crossing, rice paddies, and on the premium track a natural tunnel and a waterfall. Ride single, or tandem to share.

BY THE DAY, FOR CONFIDENT RIDERS

A classic Vespa

A classic Vespa for the day, to potter the back lanes and the rice road at your own pace. For those who already ride, with the helmets fitted and an unhurried handover.

BY THE DAY, FOR CONFIDENT RIDERS

The Honda Super Cub

The classic step-through, light and forgiving underhand, for a gentle day out on two wheels. An easy companion to the Vespa for a second rider or a gentler hand.

QUIET GROUND, AND THE ISLAND FROM THE AIR

The still days, and one from above.

A MORNING

A water temple purification

A quiet melukat purification in the cool spring water at a temple, guided gently and with respect. A still, cleansing morning, best taken early before the day warms.

A HALF DAY

A waterfall in the hills

Down through the green to a hidden fall, the cool pool, the sound of the water, and time to simply sit. Unhurried, and back before the heat.

A PRIVATE FLIGHT, BY ARRANGEMENT

The island from the air

The island seen from the air, held to its natural wonders rather than the coast's crowds. A private flight for your group alone, the terraces or the volcanoes or the islands off the coast, shaped around the light.

HOW IT IS ARRANGED

Private, and shaped around you.

One adventure on an open week, or a few woven through a journey, whichever suits.
