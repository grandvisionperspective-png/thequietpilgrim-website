A QUIET PILGRIM SERVICE

Pilgrim Transport.

The drive from Denpasar should be the first hour of the holiday, not the last hour of the journey.

WHAT IT IS

The driver who does not speak when the road is quiet.

Private drivers, airport transfers, day tours. Long-time Balinese drivers we have ridden with for years. Comfortable cars, cool water in the back, the radio off unless you turn it on.

THE ROUTES

A few shapes the road takes.

From the arrivals hall to the far coasts, with the same quiet driver.

ARRIVAL

Airport pickup

The driver is inside arrivals with your name on a board, watching your flight rather than the clock. Cool water, cold towels, the radio off unless you want it.

DEPARTURE

Airport drop-off

He arrives at the time you agreed the night before and takes you comfortably to the airport. You reach the gate unhurried.

FIVE TO SIX HOURS

Half a day

A driver for the morning or the afternoon, yours. A market run, a temple, the beach. He waits, or moves with you.

EIGHT TO TEN HOURS

A full day

From the villa in the morning and back by evening. The road is his to read, the day is yours to shape.

A LONG DAY, AN EARLY START

A North Bali day

Up into the cool highlands. An old royal temple, lunch above the rice terraces, Ulun Danu Beratan resting on its lake, and a waterfall before the road home.

A FULL DAY, THE QUIETER SIDE

An East Bali day

Mount Agung holding the skyline, the Sidemen valley and its weavers, the water palace at Tirta Gangga, and the old salt makers on the Amed coast.

THE CARS

Four ways to be carried.

Tell us how many of you there are and how much you are bringing, and we match the car. Every one comes with a driver we have ridden with for years, fuel, parking and cool water taken care of.

A COUPLE, LIGHT

City car

an everyday car

Up to four of you, three bags. For the airport run and the days around Ubud.

THE USUAL

MPV

Toyota Innova

Up to six, four bags. The car most of our weeks ride in, cool and quiet on the long roads.

THE WHOLE HOUSE

Group van

Toyota Hiace

Up to twelve, eight bags. Everyone in one car for the day out, and the same driver throughout.

A QUIETER RIDE

VIP

Toyota Alphard

Up to six, four bags. Reclining seats, the softest ride on the island, for the arrival you want to sleep through.

BY THE HOUR

A few hours, when a day is more than you need

From three hours upward. The same car and the same driver the entire time, so there is no waiting on an app and no new face each trip.

ONE WAY, SOUTH

Down to Seminyak, Canggu, or the beaches

A comfortable one way with a driver who knows the back roads when the traffic builds.

ONE WAY, SANUR

Across to Sanur and the quieter coast

An easy one way at your own hour, for the boat to the islands or a few slow days by the water.

WHO DRIVES YOU

Drivers we have ridden with for years.

Balinese, English-speaking, calm. They know the village shortcuts, the back roads when traffic builds, and the warungs that are actually good. They will recommend nothing they have not themselves visited.

PAIR THIS WITH

The week between the rides.
