A QUIET PILGRIM SERVICE

Pilgrim Journeys.

Five days, gently. We write the rhythm. You move it around.

WHAT IT IS

An itinerary, written by hand.

A single day out, or a slow week, planned around your days. A morning exploring a local market with our chef. An afternoon by the river. A cycling day through the paddies. A sound bath. Quiet meals at the long table. The road is part of the holiday, not the last hour of it.

A DAY OUT

Just the one day, if that is all you want.

A journey can be a single day, with the rest of the week left open, or the slow week further down. More than thirty days to choose from, in three families. A few of them here.

MAKERS AND CRAFTS

An artisan, and the thing made by hand.

A working compound rather than a showroom, and someone who has done the same craft most of a life.

A MORNING

Silver at a Celuk bench

You sit at a bench a family has worked for three generations, draw the silver by hand over a small flame, and leave with the small thing you made.

A MORNING

A morning at the wheel

Wet clay, a kick wheel, and a village that has shaped earth longer than anyone remembers. You throw your own and leave it to dry.

A MORNING

Coffee and chocolate at the source

The growers, the red cherries and the cacao pods, the drying and the roasting, the cup and the bar made by the people who farm them.

FIRST LIGHT

Salt drawn by hand

Early on the black sand pans, you carry the seawater the way the family has for generations and skim the first salt off the top, still warm.

CULTURE AND CEREMONY

The market, the family kitchen, and the temple.

The everyday devotion that runs under everything here, done at the kitchen table rather than staged.

BACK BY LUNCH

A morning at a local market

You walk a local market with our chef at first light, choose what looks good, and the cooking follows it home.

A MORNING

Cooking with a Balinese family

Market first, then a family kitchen, the spice paste ground by hand on a stone, and the meal eaten together. The everyday food of the island.

FIRST LIGHT

A blessing at a quiet spring

At a spring the crowds do not reach, a priest leads the water blessing spout by spout, in order. A rite, not a swim.

AN EVENING

Kecak in Junjungan

A village where one from each family performs by firelight, and the takings keep the temple standing. More performers than audience.

SCENIC AND VILLAGE DAYS

The slow road, the water, and the old villages.

Days for walking and looking rather than ticking off sights, with a driver who knows when to stop.

A DAY

The rice terraces at Jatiluwih

The wide terraces on the western slopes, quieter than the ones the buses stop at. A slow walk, the old water channels, a warung lunch with the valley below.

A DAY

A day in the Sidemen valley

The east as Ubud once was. Terraces, home looms where the cloth is still woven by hand, and Mount Agung over the fields.

A DAY

The Munduk highlands

Cool cloud forest in the north, a tall narrow fall the buses miss, coffee and clove on the slopes. A day to walk and cool down.

A LONG DAY

A day on the Amed coast

The far east, black sand fishing hamlets and painted boats, Agung behind. The quiet the south has lost.

Write to us with the days that draw you, or tell us the feeling you are after and we choose for you. Every day is arranged privately, around your week.

OR, THE WHOLE WEEK

The Bali Slow Pilgrim.

DAY ONE

Arrival, easing in

Driver meets you at Denpasar. Slow road to the villa. Arrival night dinner, plated, Pilgrim Kitchen.

DAY TWO

A slow morning

Floating breakfast on the pool. Open afternoon. Balinese home dinner, what we cook for ourselves on a Tuesday.

DAY THREE

Market morning, sound bath afternoon

Walk a local market with our chef. Sound bath, ninety minutes, at the open-air bale. Light wellness lunch, early to bed.

DAY FOUR

Cycling through the paddies

Bicycles meet at the villa gate at six. Cycle Tegallalang to Pakerisan. Coffee at a warung. Mediterranean Table dinner, long table, candle stubs.

DAY FIVE

Farewell, slowly

Departure brunch. Sun is high by the time you finish. Driver to the airport.

Nothing is fixed. Everything can move.
