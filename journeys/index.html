A QUIET PILGRIM SERVICE

Pilgrim Journeys.

Five days, gently. We write the rhythm. You move it around.

WHAT IT IS

An itinerary, written by hand.

Five-day curated villa experiences. A morning at Sukawati market with Fanny. An afternoon by the river. Cycling through paddies. A sound bath. Three quiet meals at the long table. The road is part of the holiday, not the last hour of it.

A SAMPLE WEEK

The Bali Slow Pilgrim.

DAY ONE

Arrival, easing in

Driver meets you at Denpasar. Slow road to the villa. Arrival night dinner, plated, Moon & Spoon Kitchen.

DAY TWO

A slow morning

Floating breakfast on the pool. Open afternoon. Balinese home dinner, what we cook for ourselves on a Tuesday.

DAY THREE

Sukawati morning, sound bath afternoon

Walk Sukawati market with Fanny. Sound bath, ninety minutes, at the open-air bale. Light wellness lunch, early to bed.

DAY FOUR

Cycling through the paddies

Bicycles meet at the villa gate at six. Cycle Tegallalang to Pakerisan. Coffee at a warung. Mediterranean Table dinner, long table, candle stubs.

DAY FIVE

Farewell, slowly

Departure brunch. Sun is high by the time you finish. Driver to the airport.

Nothing is fixed. Everything can move.
