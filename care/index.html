A QUIET PILGRIM SERVICE

Pilgrim Care.

You came here as parents. We make sure you also get to come here as a couple.

WHAT IT IS

A parental hand on the week.

Childcare and family support, in your villa, by people we have known for years. Balinese, English-speaking, gentle. So that some afternoons are yours, and some mornings are theirs.

HOW WE HELP

A few shapes the days can take.

AFTERNOON

An afternoon with the children

Three to four hours, in the villa, while you go for a long lunch or a sound bath. They will be drawing, playing in the pool, or quiet in the shade.

DINNER

An evening with the children

From bath time through bedtime. Dinner is cooked. You eat at the long table, in another room. The villa stays calm.

FULL DAY

A full day of family support

Morning to evening. The nanny is part of the rhythm. Beach trips, markets, naps. We work with what the day asks for.

THROUGHOUT

The whole week, gently

A trusted face from day one. Mornings start with someone the children already know. Mostly background, on hand when needed.

AROUND THE FAMILY

The rest of the house, quietly handled.

A few of the small things that make a week with children lighter, arranged before you land and taken away when you leave.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Everything the baby needs, already there

A travel cot made up, a pram, a high chair by the table, a steriliser, a monitor, and a pool fence where the villa asks for one. Delivered and set up before you land, collected at the end, so none of it fills your own luggage.

MOST MORNINGS

Laundry, quietly returned

A bag left by the door in the morning comes back clean, pressed and folded, with a same-day turn when a dinner or a flight asks for it.

LONGER STAYS

An extra pair of hands about the house

For a fuller house or a longer week. A second clean through the day, beds changed and towels refreshed, the kitchen reset after a big lunch, and a hand with the small errands.

WHO COMES TO YOUR VILLA

Known to us, for years.

Every nanny on our roster has worked with our own families and friends. Balinese, soft-spoken, English-speaking, with first-aid certification and references we can show you.

No agency in the middle. You meet them on arrival. If it is not the right fit, we change, simply.
