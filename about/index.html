ABOUT

Two people, five services, one village.

Fanny in the kitchen. Barrie on the road. The team that makes the week, makes the week.

WHY THIS EXISTS

The traveler who has done loud holidays and wants the opposite.

We started The Quiet Pilgrim in 2023 because the guests we kept hearing from were tired of hospitality that performed itself. They wanted the small things done well, the loud things turned down, and a real person on the other end of every booking. That is what we make.

THE TEAM

The hands behind the week.

FANNY

Chef, Moon & Spoon Kitchen

Trained in Indonesian and European technique. Has cooked through villa kitchens in Ubud for years. Reads the morning before she reads the menu. Replies to email before the kitchen wakes up.

BARRIE

Pilgrim Journeys, Care, Transport, Wellness

Writes the itineraries. Holds the calendar. Knows which driver is on, which nanny is right for which children, which sound healer is in town this week. The road, the route, the rest.

TEAM

Hand-picked, on call

Drivers, nannies, wellness practitioners, occasional second-chefs. Every one of them known to us for years. References on file. None of them subcontracted from an agency.
