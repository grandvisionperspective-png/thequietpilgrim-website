A QUIET PILGRIM SERVICE

Pilgrim Transport.

The drive from Denpasar should be the first hour of the holiday, not the last hour of the journey.

WHAT IT IS

The driver who does not speak when the road is quiet.

Private drivers, airport transfers, day tours. Long-time Balinese drivers we have ridden with for years. Comfortable cars, cool water in the back, the radio off unless you turn it on.

THE ROUTES

A few shapes the road takes.

ARRIVAL

Airport pickup

The driver is at arrivals before you walk out. Cool water, cold towels. The drive is quiet unless you want it otherwise.

DEPARTURE

Airport drop-off

We leave with time. Your driver arrives at the check-out time you agreed the night before, and takes you comfortably to the airport. You arrive at the gate unhurried.

DAY

A day with the driver

For market mornings, temple visits, the cycling drop, the beach. The driver waits or moves with you.

NORTH

A North Bali day

Up into the cool highlands. The old royal temple, lunch above the rice terraces, Ulun Danu Beratan resting on its lake, a waterfall at Gitgit or Sekumpul, and the natural hot springs to end the day. Back by evening.

EAST

An East Bali day

East toward the sacred side of the island. Mount Agung on the skyline, Besakih the mother temple on its slopes, the royal water palace at Tirta Gangga, and Lempuyang, the mountain temple with its gate to the sky. A quieter Bali.

WHO DRIVES YOU

Drivers we have ridden with for years.

Balinese, English-speaking, calm. They know the village shortcuts, the back roads when traffic builds, and the warungs that are actually good. They will recommend nothing they have not themselves visited.

The week between the rides.
