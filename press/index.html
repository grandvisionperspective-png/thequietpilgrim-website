BOILERPLATE

For the fact sheet.

The Quiet Pilgrim is a bespoke hospitality umbrella based in Ubud, working across Bali. Founded 2023, it brings together five hospitality services (Pilgrim Kitchen by Moon & Spoon, Pilgrim Journeys, Pilgrim Care, Pilgrim Transport, Pilgrim Wellness) under one quietly run team. Each service is run directly, transparently priced, and built around the idea that a guest's time in Bali should be slow, considered, and uncrowded by hospitality theatre.
