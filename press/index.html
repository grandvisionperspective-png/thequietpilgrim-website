BOILERPLATE

For the fact sheet.

The Quiet Pilgrim is a bespoke hospitality umbrella in Ubud, Bali. Founded 2023 by Fanny and Barrie, it brings together five small services (Moon & Spoon Kitchen, Pilgrim Journeys, Pilgrim Care, Pilgrim Transport, Pilgrim Wellness) under one quietly run team. Each service is hand-curated, transparently priced, and built around the idea that a guest's time in Ubud should be slow, considered, and uncrowded by hospitality theatre.
